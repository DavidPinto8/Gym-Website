Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to book classes in advance?

Yes, our classes fill up quickly. We recommend booking your spot through your profile at least 24 hours in advance to keep your routine steady.

Can I check if the gym is crowded right now?

Absolutely. As a Pro or Elite member, you can log into your account and check the real-time availability of equipment on the main floor.

Can I cancel my membership at any time?

Our Starter and Pro plans are contract-free and can be canceled with a 30-day notice. The Elite plan requires a 6-month commitment.
